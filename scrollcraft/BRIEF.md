## The eight topics

1. **Vibe**: fresh, weightless, confident. Blue as weather, not decoration. Refs: Apple
   product-page restraint, the first cool day of fall, graph paper.
2. **Journey**: hero (the melon floats, your quarter at a glance) → the StudentVUE ritual →
   one sign in and everything appears → the regrade (scroll is the slider) → the rest of the
   toolkit → commitment.
3. **Energy**: calm open, mild dread, lift, confident peak, appetite, quiet resolve.
4. **Feeling + peak**: see the curve below. Peak: "you scroll and the test score climbs and
   the grade flips from B to A, live, with the math in front of you."
5. **One thing no site does**: the scroll-regrade. Scrolling is the slider on a real grade
   calculator running ojjyMelon's actual letter-grade math.
6. **Range**: playful-premium. Illustrated, weightless, rounded, one blue world.
7. **Structure**: distinct acts, not one unbroken world. It is an argument, not a place.
8. **Assets**: existing brand SVGs (melon mark, avatars), Manrope, the app's own components.
   No photography, no footage. **The world is illustrated on purpose**: the brand's identity
   is already flat vector (logo, avatars), so the illustrated-world exception in worlds.md
   applies. No generated media, no scrub clips.

## What it is, who for

A free StudentVUE client for MCPS students. For anyone who checks grades weekly and wants
them readable at speed.

## What the visitor must believe by the end

Checking your grades can feel weightless instead of like data entry.

## The one action

Get started. Same label in the hero, the close, and the top bar.

## Grammar: filmic one-shot, with the burden of proof met

One linear argument, one emotional arc, one CTA label, no chapters, no jump points. Why the
other seven lost: chaptered editorial is print-shaped and this is a tool; live surface fits
two acts but not the whole page; continuous world needs geography and this has none;
typographic poster would throw away real product visuals; gallery is a range with no
argument; split stage has only one comparative beat; cutlist is the wrong energy for calm
confidence. Deviations from the template skeleton (the anti-template-trap moves): no scrub
clips at all (illustrated world, no footage), the app's real top bar instead of a minimal
marketing bar, an illustrated layered hero instead of a full-bleed photo scrub, two
live-surface acts running the product's own logic, a bespoke regrade peak, and a colophon
close carrying authorship credits.

## Feeling curve

```
1  Weightless        the melon floats in open blue, planes sliding at different rates
2  Familiar dread    the ritual lines land one by one, each one true
3  Relief            a wipe and the whole gradebook is just there, already filled in
4  Confidence (PEAK) the score climbs under your hand and the letter flips B to A
5  Appetite          the rest of the toolkit slides past sideways
6  Resolve           everything stops, one melon, one button, a colophon
```

No two adjacent acts share a feeling. The peak has the largest span on the page and the
quietest act (relief) directly before it.

## The peak

Sentence a visitor would say: "you scroll and the test score climbs and the grade flips
from B to A, live, with the math happening in front of you." Lives in act 4, span 3.4, the
largest on the page.

## Tell-someone sentence

"It's the site where scrolling is the slider on a real grade calculator and you watch a B
flip to an A."

## Signature move

**The scroll-regrade.** A working mini gradebook pinned mid-page. Act progress drives a
sample assignment from 16/20 to 19/20; the category bar refills; the overall percentage and
letter recompute every frame through ojjyMelon's real `letterGrade` / category math (the
same code the app ships). The letter badge snaps B to A at 89.5 and back when you scroll
up. Sample data, labelled on its face; the computation is the product's own.
